📚 About

Mizanya is a personal spending tracker for Bahrain. Your bank already texts you for every card payment and transfer; Mizanya reads those messages, works out what each payment was, and tells you every day how much you can still spend until payday.

There is no account and no bank login. Everything is processed and stored on your iPhone.

⚡ Features

💬 Automatic capture

Bank SMS arrive in Mizanya by themselves through a one-time Shortcuts automation.

🎯 Daily allowance

One number each day: what you can spend and still hit your monthly target.

🏷 Smart categories

200+ Bahraini merchants recognised automatically. Every correction becomes a rule.

📊 Monthly report

Donut chart, tap any category for every payment, and where you overspent and why.

🤝 Friends paying back

Fawri+ repayments after a shared bill are matched, so you see only your share.

🔄 Own transfers & refunds

Moving money between your own accounts and refunds never count as spending.

🔔 Notifications

Payment logged, “What was this?” with one-tap answers, budget alerts, payday.

🌎 Bilingual

Full English and Arabic support with right-to-left layout, light and dark mode.

🔧 Setup

iOS does not let apps read your messages, so Mizanya uses Apple's Shortcuts app. The in-app guide shows every screen; this is the summary.

1. Add the Mizanya shortcut (one tap)

  1. In Mizanya, tap Add the Mizanya shortcut, read what it does, then tap Open in Shortcuts.
  2. Tap Add Shortcut. It has one step: Log Shortcut Input in Mizanya.

2. Create one automation per bank

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Message.
  2. Sender: your bank, spelled exactly as in Messages (e.g. AlSalamBank, BisB). Leave “Message Contains” empty.
  3. On the automation page, tap the row under Do and pick Mizanya SMS under My Shortcuts.
  4. Set Run Immediately, then tap Done.

Using two banks? Add the shortcut once, then repeat part 2 for each bank.

🏦 Supported banks

Al Salam Bank and Bahrain Islamic Bank (BisB). More Bahraini banks are planned.

Mizanya is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any bank, Benefit or Apple. Bank names are used only to describe which SMS formats are supported.

❓ FAQ

What devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Newer iOS versions get extra touches such as Liquid Glass on iOS 26.

Can Mizanya read my other messages?

No. Only messages from the bank you choose as Sender in your automation reach Mizanya. One-time password messages are recognised and discarded without being saved.

Does Mizanya connect to my bank?

No. It never asks for bank passwords or card numbers. It only reads the SMS your bank already sends you.

Nothing arrives after a payment. What should I check?

Check the Sender spelling in your automation (one wrong letter stops it), that Do is set to the Mizanya SMS shortcut, and that the automation is set to Run Immediately.

A box asking for “Message” pops up.

The automation's Do is set to the “Log Bank SMS” tile instead of the Mizanya SMS shortcut. Pick the shortcut instead.

Can I try it without setting anything up?

Yes. On the Home screen tap “Try with sample data”. It loads made-up transactions you can remove any time in Settings.

How do I stop it or remove my data?

Delete the automation or the Mizanya SMS shortcut in the Shortcuts app. In Mizanya, Settings › Delete all data removes everything.

How do I report a bug or a message that isn't recognised?

Email us with a short description. If an SMS isn't recognised, include its text with balances and account digits removed.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Everything stays on your iPhone. Mizanya has no server, no account and no sign-in, and collects no data. Nothing about you or your spending is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no tracking, advertising or analytics.

What Mizanya processes

If you set up the optional automation in Apple's Shortcuts app, the text of SMS messages from the bank senders you choose is passed to Mizanya. Mizanya reads the amount, merchant, date, the last digits of the account and the balance, and saves the transaction on your device only. You can also paste bank messages yourself and add categories and notes.

Mizanya does not read any of your other messages; does not ask for or store bank passwords, card numbers, PINs or one-time codes (one-time-password messages are discarded without being saved); does not connect to your bank; and does not send any of this information over the internet.

Where your data is stored

Transactions, categories, rules and settings are stored in Mizanya's private storage on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings, which Apple encrypts and controls.

On-device intelligence

On supported iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Mizanya may ask Apple's on-device model to suggest a category for an unknown merchant name. This runs entirely on your iPhone; Mizanya does not send the merchant name to us or to any server.

Notifications

All notifications are created on your iPhone. No notification content passes through any server.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data removes every transaction and rule immediately. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. To stop new messages reaching Mizanya, delete the automation or the Mizanya SMS shortcut in the Shortcuts app.

Children

Mizanya is not directed at children under 13.
