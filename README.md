## About

Your bank already sends you an SMS for every card payment and transfer. Mizanya reads those messages, categorises each payment and tells you every day how much you can still spend until payday — privately, on your iPhone. No account, no bank login, no server.

## Features

- **Automatic capture** of bank SMS through a one-time Shortcuts automation
- **Daily allowance**: what you can spend today and still hit your monthly target
- **Smart categories**: 200+ Bahraini merchants recognised; your corrections become rules
- **Monthly report** with a tap-to-drill-down donut chart and overspending insights
- **Friends paying you back**: Fawri+ repayments matched to shared bills
- **Own-account transfers and refunds** excluded from spending
- **Notifications**: payments, “What was this?” with one-tap answers, budget alerts, payday
- **Bilingual**: full English and Arabic with right-to-left layout; light and dark mode

## Supported Banks

Al Salam Bank and Bahrain Islamic Bank (BisB). Mizanya is not affiliated with any bank, Benefit or Apple.
